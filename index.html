How it works

Three steps, about a minute

Nothing is stored until you ask for an analysis, and you can start over at any point.

  1. 01

    Upload a front-facing photo

    A phone photo in daylight is enough. We resize it on your device before it is sent, and delete it after the analysis.

  2. 02

    Read your analysis and pick a cut

    You get your face shape, hair texture and colour, plus five or six cuts with a one-line reason each. Preview any of them on your own photo.

  3. 03

    Find a barber who does it

    Search by ZIP code or city. Each shop shows its rating, hours, phone and website, so you can call or book with them directly.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What happens to my photo?

It is resized in your browser, sent once for the analysis, and not kept on our servers afterwards. The result is saved on your device so you can come back to it; “Start over” clears it.

Why credits instead of a subscription for clients?

Most people need LineUp a few times a year, not every month. Each analysis and preview costs us a real fee from the model providers, so you pay per use and credits never expire.

Where do the barbershops come from?

From public listings and ratings. No shop pays to appear, and nothing changes the order. Each result shows the shop's hours, phone, map and website.

Can I book through LineUp?

Not yet. Each result links to the shop's phone, map and website, so you book with them directly and pay at the shop. Booking inside LineUp is on the way.

Can I get a refund on credits?

If an analysis or preview fails on our side, the credits go straight back to your balance. Unused packs can be refunded within 14 days of purchase; write to support with your receipt.
